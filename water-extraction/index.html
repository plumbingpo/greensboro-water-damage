Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across Greensboro, Greensboro Restoration handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Stairwells or window wells that fill up and spill water inside
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Make sure your home has working water leak sensors in critical areas
  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Maintain appliance supply lines and replace hoses that show wear or age
